Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 23, 2026

At Droplink, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to ensure your personal data is protected.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate and protect our subdomain forwarding service:

  • Discord Account Data: When you login, we retrieve your Discord User ID, username, display name, avatar, and email address. We use this information to create and verify your account.
  • DNS Configurations: We store the subdomain name, record type (A, CNAME, etc.), and destination IP/hostname you assign to your subdomains. This is necessary to configure records in Cloudflare.
  • Server Logs: We log standard technical request details (IP address, browser type) to debug service errors and prevent abuse of our API.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To set up and manage your Droplink account.
  • To synchronise your custom subdomains with the Cloudflare DNS API.
  • To enforce resource limits (e.g. 3 active records per free account).
  • To block malicious users, spam, and bot activity.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, lease, or distribute your personal data. We only share data with the following services to run the platform:

  • Cloudflare API: Subdomain definitions (FQDMs, IPs, types) are sent to Cloudflare to configure DNS routing and proxying.
  • Hosting Providers: Our database is hosted securely on our servers to store active sessions and user records.

4. Cookies and Sessions

We use simple, secure cookies to maintain your login session. These cookies do not track your activity across other sites and are used solely for authentication purposes.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated DNS records at any time. Simply contact us or clear your subdomains in the dashboard and delete your account from your settings page.
